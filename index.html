Equations, parameters and scope

The pattern is cos(pπx/L) cos(qπz/h), with L = 12 mm and h = 2 mm. Its amplitude follows 10 exp(−λt) mV. The mean follows 5 exp(−t/10 ms) mV.

For k = (pπ/L, qπ/h), aᵢ = kᵀΣᵢk and aₑ = kᵀΣₑk. The bidomain symbol is qB = aᵢaₑ/(aᵢ+aₑ). The selected local monodomain tensor has directional entries σᵢσₑ/(σᵢ+σₑ), giving qM = kᵀΣₘk. The decay rate is λ = 1/τ + q/(βCm).

β = 140 mm⁻¹, Cm = 0.01 μF/mm² and τ = 10 ms. The tensors are constant and co-aligned. Both compartments have no normal boundary current. The extracellular mean is zero. This is an evaluation of a closed-form solution.
